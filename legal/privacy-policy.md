# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [Effective date]

This Privacy Policy explains what Money Backers ("we", "us") collects, why, and what you can do about it. Money Backers is operated by [Company legal name], [Company address]. Questions: [contact email].

## The short version

- Your receipts stay **in your own browser**. We don't keep them on our servers.
- If you join the waitlist, we store your **email address** and a referral code.
- We don't sell your data, and we don't show ads.
- You can delete your data any time.

## What we collect today

### 1. Waitlist signups
When you join the waitlist we store:
- your email address,
- a random 8-character referral code we create for you,
- the referral code of the person who invited you (if any),
- the date and time you signed up.

We use this to tell you when Money Backers launches, to count referrals, and to give out referral rewards. We store it in a file on our server.

### 2. Receipts you add in the app
- **Pasted text:** the text is sent to our server to be read (store, date, total) and the result is sent straight back. We don't save it.
- **PDF uploads:** the file is sent to our server, read in memory, and the result is sent back. The file is not saved to disk or kept.
- **Photos:** text is read from photos **on your own device**, inside your browser. The photo is not sent to our server. To do this, your browser downloads a free text-reading tool (Tesseract.js) from a public download service (jsDelivr). That service can see your IP address, as any website you load can.
- **Saved receipts:** your receipt list and results are saved in your browser's local storage on your device. We cannot see them. Clearing your browser data, or tapping **"Delete all my data"** in the app, removes them.

### 3. Things stored on your device
Your browser keeps your receipts, your waitlist referral code, and the code of whoever referred you. We don't use advertising cookies or tracking pixels.

### 4. Server logs
Like most websites, our server and hosting provider ([Hosting provider, e.g. Render]) may automatically record basic technical info such as IP address, browser type, and the pages you requested, for security and fixing problems. [Log retention period].

## What we don't do
- We don't sell or rent your personal information.
